# SciFi Sound Design Pack — Volume 1

A collection of **168 original stereo WAV sound effects** created by Clint Wagoner for science-fiction games, films, trailers, animations, podcasts, music, and interactive media.

## Contents

- Atmospheric sci-fi beds and tension-building strikes
- Spaceship flybys, flyovers, interiors, startups, power sequences, and undocking effects
- Blasters, plasma cannons, railguns, pulse rifles, and other futuristic weapons
- Glitches, electronic effects, impacts, and experimental textures

## Technical details

- **168 stereo WAV files**
- **Approximately 26 minutes, 45 seconds of audio**
- **16-bit and 24-bit audio**
- **44.1, 48, 96, and 192 kHz sample rates**
- Filenames use the `SciFi-V1_` prefix

The files are compatible with standard DAWs, video editors, Unity, Unreal Engine, and other software that imports WAV audio.

## Quick start

1. Copy the sounds into your project or media library.
2. Browse by category and descriptive filename.
3. Edit, layer, process, and synchronize the sounds within your creative project.
4. Keep the original library and this license together in your archive.
